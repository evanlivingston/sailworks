Race day
========

Sailworks supports the planning and review side of racing: studying the forecast,
building a route, bringing instruments onto the chart, and comparing what the boat
actually did with what the polar predicted. It is a planning and analysis aid, not a
primary navigation system.

Today's race day fits into four stages.

Prepare ashore
--------------

- Download the forecast for the race area and time window in
  **File → Data Library… → Forecasts**.
- Load or import the boat's polar in **Polar manager**, and check that it is the
  configuration you will sail.
- Build a route in the **Routing** panel, or set up tracks and marks you want on the
  chart. Import GPX files if the race documents one.
- Check the instrument connection in **Settings → Connections**, and run the simulator
  if you want to confirm the app reads data before leaving.
- Review the [charts and weather](charts-weather.md) for the area and the
  [routing](routing.md) limits you plan to use.

Before leaving
--------------

- Reload the latest forecast; forecasts issued closer to the start usually differ from
  the one you used ashore.
- Recalculate the route with the final departure time and check each leg against
  official charts.
- Start recording in **Logs** before the boat leaves the dock if you want a complete
  record of the day.
- Confirm that heading, wind, speed and position are updating.

While sailing
-------------

- Follow the route on the chart and use the time controls to step through the forecast.
- Keep the NMEA panel on **Live data source** so the instruments and performance overlay
  keep collecting.
- Treat the route as guidance. Wind shifts, current and traffic can make a different
  course faster or safer.

After finishing
---------------

- Stop and save the recording in **Logs** so the session is kept.
- Open **Performance** in Polars and compare measured speed with the predicted curves.
- Export tracks or routes with **File → Export navigation…** if you want to keep them
  outside the app.
- Note what differed from the plan: forecast timing, wind strength, current, or boat
  setup.

Future direction — not available
--------------------------------

Sailworks does not currently provide full race management or real-time racing features.
Ideas such as live race tracking, starting-line and timer workflows, and automated race
scoring are a direction for the product, not committed or scheduled functionality. Do
not plan a race around them.

Unverified
----------

Race and YB workflow support that may exist in the application has not been verified for
operational use. No instructions are given here because the behavior, formats and
reliability are unconfirmed. Use the general forecast, routing and logging features
described above instead.
